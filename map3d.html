Twenty miles around the house

The house sits at about 800 feet on the flank of Storm King. Within 20 miles the land runs from the Hudson at sea level to about 1,670 feet on Schunemunk Mountain, six miles to the southwest.

Places from the guide

    Terrain: AWS Terrain Tiles, built from USGS 3DEP elevation data (about 10-meter cells here); the contour lines are computed from the same data. The LiDAR relief layer is the USGS 3D Elevation Program's 1-meter bare-earth model (around the house it comes from a 2022 flight; other counties in the circle were flown in other years), shaded by USGS; it shows the ground with trees and buildings stripped away, which is why old stone walls, cellar holes, roads and quarry cuts show up under the forest. Buildings, roads, trails, peaks and the smaller points of interest: OpenStreetMap contributors, served by OpenFreeMap. Most buildings carry no height in OpenStreetMap and are drawn at a standard height, so they show where things are, not how tall they are. Aerial photo: Esri World Imagery. The house is placed at the center of its tax parcel; guide places use the guide's own coordinates, which are approximate.
